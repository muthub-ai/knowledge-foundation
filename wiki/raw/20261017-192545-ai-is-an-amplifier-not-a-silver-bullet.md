# AI is an amplifier, not a silver bullet

Published 2025-09-24. Adapted from my LinkedIn post of September 23, 2025.

**AI does not fix a team; it amplifies whatever is already there.** The [2025 DORA report](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-the-2025-dora-report) put data behind what many of us had seen in practice: strong teams get stronger, and struggling teams find their existing problems highlighted and intensified. The greatest return came not from the AI tools themselves but from the quality of internal platforms, the clarity of workflows and the alignment of teams.

That matches my experience. When we moved hundreds of data scientists onto one governed ML platform, the speedup came less from any clever tool and more from removing the dozens of small, inconsistent paths to production. Give AI a messy delivery system and it produces a mess at higher volume.

Two other points from the research deserve more attention than they get. First, DORA recommends clarifying and socializing your AI policies. In my experience a clear policy is a blueprint, not a blockade: teams experiment more, not less, when they know where the lines are. Second, acceleration exposes weaknesses downstream. Faster coding only helps if testing, version control and feedback loops keep up. Otherwise you have moved the queue and called it productivity.

Before buying another assistant, ask what it will amplify in your organization today.
