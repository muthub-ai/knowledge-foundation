# Overview

For complex work in regulated sectors, prompt-to-code cannot pass an audit. Making the specification the contract an AI must fulfil can, provided the spec stays anchored to the code and machines check conformance.[^s1]

# What it covers

- Why prompt-to-code fails the audit test
- What changes in the lifecycle
- The honest critique
- Spec-anchored, not spec-as-source
- Why spec programs decay
- Make conformance mechanical

# Bottom line

Make the spec the contract, keep it anchored to the code, and let machines prove conformance. That is the balance regulated industries need between innovation and control.
