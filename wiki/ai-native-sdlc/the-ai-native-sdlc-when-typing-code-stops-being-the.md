# Overview

AI has made writing code cheap. Verification, trusted context and human judgment are now the scarce resources, and teams that redesign their lifecycle around them will pull away from teams that only add assistants.[^s1]

# What it covers

- The evidence is more awkward than the demos
- Spend the saved time on better problems
- A lifecycle that remembers
- Architecture that agents can execute
- Verification becomes the senior job
- Measure flow and trust, not output
- The foundation is trusted knowledge

# Bottom line

Code fast, verify hard, and keep the humans in the loop. The real investment is trusted knowledge and controls that live inside the engineering flow, not an approval step bolted on at the end.
