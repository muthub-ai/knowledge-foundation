# Overview

A trained model is the start of the work, not the end. The lessons of industrializing machine learning carry straight into generative and agentic AI, which is why AI engineering has to unify DataOps, MLOps and DevOps.[^s1]

# What it covers

- Why ML was never just software
- The lifecycle we industrialized
- What carries over to generative AI
- What does not carry over
- From MLOps to AI engineering
- The head start nobody talks about

# Bottom line

The lessons of MLOps still hold for generative and agentic AI: industrialize the path to production, measure what happens after launch, and give risk teams the same evidence the builders have.
