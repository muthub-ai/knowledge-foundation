# Overview

Most enterprise AI pilots hit the same wall: systems record what happened, but the reasoning behind decisions evaporates. Durable, governed context about the why is the real foundation for useful agents.[^s1]

# What it covers

- Enterprise amnesia
- Systems of record keep the what
- Context engineering needs something to engineer
- A living document that lies is worse than none
- Design choices that make knowledge durable
- A decision trace, worked through
- Where to start

# Bottom line

If your agent cannot produce the evidence, policy version, tool actions and decision context behind a high-stakes action, you do not yet have an auditable agent. The teams that pull ahead will be the ones that build durable institutional memory, whatever model they happen to run.
