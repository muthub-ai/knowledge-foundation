# Overview

In a large organization you rarely own the teams you need to move. Architects lead through clarity, credibility and standards people want to follow, and by treating the North Star and today's fires as one job.[^s1]

# What it covers

- The real risk is misalignment
- North Star and firefighting are one job
- Credibility is a currency you spend
- Make the compliant path the easy path
- When influence runs out
- What has worked for me

# Bottom line

Authority is granted. Influence is earned, through clarity, practical standards and results people can see, and it is spent every time you say no, so spend it carefully.
