# Overview

Documentation fails because it is a second job. The knowledge we need is already produced as a by-product of work: commits, tickets, incidents, reviews and decisions. Capture it passively, distill it, and keep a human approval gate before anything becomes trusted.[^s1]

# What it covers

- The exhaust of work
- Passive, not automatic
- Why the human gate matters
- A feedback loop, not a pipeline
- An incident, end to end
- Security, privacy and restraint
- How to know it is working
- Where to start

# Bottom line

Stop asking people to document. Capture the knowledge their work already produces, distill it with agents, and keep a human approval gate before it becomes trusted. Knowledge that writes itself, with people who sign for it.
