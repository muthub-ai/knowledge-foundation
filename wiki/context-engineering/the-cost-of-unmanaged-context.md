# Overview

Enterprises budget for compute, models and licenses, but almost none budget for the context those models depend on. Unmanaged context is the largest cost nobody tracks, and every new AI agent adds to it.[^s1]

# What it covers

- The cost we already pay
- Why AI multiplies it
- Four hidden line items
- A familiar night
- Why this is a leadership problem
- What stays yours when the models change
- What managed context looks like
- Start by measuring it

# Bottom line

Context is the asset AI runs on, and most enterprises leave it unmanaged. Build it once, govern it once, and let every model you adopt reuse it. The rest of this series covers how.
