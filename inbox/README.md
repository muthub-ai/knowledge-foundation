# Inbox

Drop one capture note per source here, then run `make ingest` on your Mac. Template: `inbox/TEMPLATE.md.txt`.

- Your own essays, posts, talks and repositories can go in full (`origin_kind: own-...`).
- Third-party material is your notes plus at most a short excerpt (`third-party-note` or `third-party-excerpt`,
  2,500 characters at most). Full copies stay in your private folder.
- `published_at` is the date the source was published, not today.
